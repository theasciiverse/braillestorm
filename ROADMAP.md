# Roadmap

What each release up to 1.0.0 brings. Versions follow the Asciiverse rule:
0.x.0 while playable but unfinished, 1.0.0 when everything planned is in.
From 0.4.0 on this is a proposal (2026-10-29), not settled yet.

## 0.2.0: built

- The ship, the warping grid, bombs, three enemy kinds (glob, cursor, fork)
- Waves with banners, the clear bonus, rushes from wave 10
- Lives, extra lives, game over, high score, the demo autopilot

## 0.3.0: built

- Sound, all synthesised
- A score multiplier up to x10
- Two more enemy kinds: the spinner and the null

## 0.4.0: pick-ups

- [ ] Pick-ups dropped by enemies now and then: more barrels, faster shots,
      a shield, an extra bomb
- [ ] Stages: every few waves a new grid colour, so a run feels like it goes
      somewhere

## 0.5.0: more of the storm

- [ ] One or two more enemy kinds of our own
- [ ] Music, synthesised, that rises with the waves

## 1.0.0

- [ ] Everything above played through and tuned
